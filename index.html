Our Core Capabilities

Linear Static FEA

What We Do

  • Stress/strain and FoS on welded, bolted, and cast parts.
  • Analyze load paths and verify model quality.
  • Perform hand calculations for rapid verification.

Deliverables

  • Hotspot map & design notes
  • Factor-of-safety table
  • Model/mesh quality checklist

Transient Dynamics

What We Do

  • Analyze time-domain response to shock and duty cycles.
  • Select appropriate solvers (explicit/implicit) and identify damping.
  • Incorporate strain-rate and contact nonlinearity.

Deliverables

  • Peak response / damage metrics
  • Critical event analysis for design review

Modal & Frequency Response

What We Do

  • Perform modal extraction and boundary condition tuning.
  • Analyze FRF / sine sweep and operational deflection shapes.
  • Provide recommendations for vibration isolation.

Deliverables

  • Mode shape tables & animations
  • Resonance avoidance playbook

Fatigue Analysis (Weld Specialization)

What We Do

  • Nominal, structural, and advanced weld fatigue analysis.
  • Create duty-cycles and perform rainflow damage calculation.
  • Recommend weld geometry and process improvements.

Deliverables

  • Fatigue life map + critical weld report
  • Quantified before/after design improvements

Multibody Dynamics & Load Extraction

What We Do

  • Develop Adams/Simulink models for events & duty cycles.
  • Extract joint/constraint forces for FEA boundary loads.
  • Run parameter sweeps to identify worst-case scenarios.

Deliverables

  • Comprehensive load case library
  • Ready-to-run FEA load decks

Structural Optimization

What We Do

  • Perform topology, size, shape, and gauge optimization.
  • Manage multi-objective trade-offs (e.g., mass vs. stiffness).
  • Incorporate manufacturing constraints (symmetry, draw, etc.).

Deliverables

  • 2-3 optimized design concepts
  • CAD-ready geometry guidance

System-Level Optimization

What We Do

  • Optimize architecture across mechanics and control systems.
  • Use surrogate models for rapid iteration and trade-off studies.
  • Handle multi-discipline constraints (NVH, thermal, etc.).

Deliverables

  • Pareto set with recommended design point
  • Parameter sensitivity analysis

Control of Mechanical Systems

What We Do

  • Model systems with linearization, observers, and friction.
  • Design robust controllers (PID, LQR, anti-windup).
  • Perform co-simulation with plant models for validation.

Deliverables

  • Tuned controller gains and parameters
  • System performance validation plots

Mechanical System Modeling

What We Do

  • Create lumped-parameter and state-space models.
  • Estimate parameters and validate models against test data.
  • Develop reduced-order models for control and optimization.

Deliverables

  • Calibrated model with documented assumptions
  • Validation plots and error analysis

Analysis-Driven CAD Improvements

What We Do

  • Propose geometry edits based on stress and fatigue analysis.
  • Provide design-for-manufacturability (DFM) feedback.
  • Quickly re-run simulations to confirm improvements.

Deliverables

  • Annotated CAD files with a change log
  • Before/after performance metrics (FoS, life, mass)

About Tepe Optimization Lab

Led by Umut Tepe, Tepe Optimization Lab (ToL) is a specialized engineering consultancy focused on Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE) and multi-disciplinary optimization. We partner with clients to solve complex mechanical challenges, enhance product performance, and reduce development time.

Operating from Milan and Istanbul, we combine rigorous analytical methods with practical design insights to deliver clear, actionable results that drive innovation and efficiency.
